01. The project evaluates customer support intent classification using OpenAI and LangSmith.
02. The goal is to understand failures, clarify routing rules and measure before-and-after performance.
03. Sources are the Evaluations Using LangSmith handout and the Gen Academy evaluation repository.
04. The dataset contains 100 synthetic tickets: 25 seed scenarios, each original plus three paraphrases.
05. The five intents are order_status, refund_request, product_issue, account_help and other.
06. OpenAI gpt-4o-mini generated structured baseline, focused and fresh v3 predictions.
07. Code-based Exact Match compares each prediction with an approved reference intent.
08. LangSmith stores experiment traces, evaluator feedback and completed human annotation queues.
09. I reviewed AI-assisted explanations, added and confirmed Excel notes in L, and approved reviews in M.
10. The original queues contain 15 completed run reviews for 14 tickets and 75 verified feedback records.

13. Under the final policy, saved baseline scored 92%, focused 94%, and fresh v3 100%.
14. V3 clarifies returned-item refund payments and unwanted-order cancellation refunds as refund_request.
15. It corrected six predictions, t024–t029, with zero regressions: a six-point gain over focused.
