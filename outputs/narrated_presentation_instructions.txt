Open customer_support_project_presentation_narrated_auto.pptx in desktop Microsoft PowerPoint. Start the Loom desktop recorder with Full Screen and Use system audio enabled; switch the microphone off for prerecorded narration. Then switch to PowerPoint and press F5 (Slide Show From Beginning). Nine slides are configured to play narration and advance automatically over 3 minutes 56.7 seconds. Slide 10 is a hidden demo guide. Do not start the separate WAV file too. Press Esc to stop the slide show. The audio is embedded; the PPTX does not require a separate audio folder. Package, media hashes, slide timings and rendered slide appearance were checked. Actual playback and timing in native desktop PowerPoint have not been tested in this environment. Make a short test recording before the final take.
