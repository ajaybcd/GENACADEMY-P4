Customer support intent evaluation final report

This project evaluates a five-intent e-commerce support classifier on 100 fixed tickets. A single routing-precedence paragraph was tested with gpt-4o-mini at temperature 0. Both fresh experiments completed without API errors. Participant failure reviews and LangSmith annotation queues are complete.

Conclusion
Under the participant-reviewed reference labels, both prompts score 93/100. The change produces seven wins and seven regressions, providing no net accuracy gain. The experiment supports a narrower next revision.

Method and prompt change

  1. Retain 100 unique IDs and fixed queries. The upload has exactly query, intent and id, mapped to input, reference output and metadata.
  2. Use the same model, temperature and required structured intent schema for both fresh runs. The classifier sees the query, not its reference.
  3. Use code-based Exact Match. Review errors, group mechanisms, and inspect wins and regressions.
  4. Complete participant failure review in the workbook and import the approved feedback into the matching LangSmith runs.

The largest baseline mechanism was refund wording overriding the underlying delivery or product problem. The only prompt change was this appended paragraph:

Routing precedence: classify the underlying problem before the requested remedy. When a refund is requested because an order never arrived, choose order_status; when it is requested because an item arrived damaged, wrong, defective, or not as described, choose product_issue. Otherwise keep the category definitions above unchanged.

Failure analysis and next experiment

  • Requested remedy overrides underlying problem: t004–t007 and t044, t045, t047 improve under reviewed labels. The baseline followed refund wording instead of the delivery/product routing policy.
  • Refund payment confused with order delivery: t024–t029 regress. A refund payment not arriving, or a cancellation request, does not establish product non-delivery.
  • Reference label policy disagreement: t046's source policy prioritizes product damage; its participant-reviewed label prioritizes the refund remedy. Original and reviewed scores are retained separately.

Next test: distinguish a missing order from a missing refund payment, preserve refund_request for cancellation/refund requests without non-delivery, and settle the product-versus-remedy policy before testing unseen ticket families. This further revision has not been run.
